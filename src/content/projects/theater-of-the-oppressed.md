Theater of the Oppressed, what is it? TO uses theater games, techniques, and exercises developed by Augusto Boal (during the military dictatorship that took place in Brazil in the 70’s) as a way to facilitate social, political, and personal transformation. It is used around the world as a form of grassroots organizing and community-based education.

While writing their thesis at the Latinx Research Center through UC Berkeley, Mylo gained the opportunity to train with Augusto Boal’s son, Julian Boal and TO expert, Geo Gritto at the Escola de Teatro Popular (School of Popular Theater) in Rio de Janeiro, in 2021. 

Mylo also included their TO research in the development of “Introductions to Art Therapy," a Decal that they co-taught with Vice Farley at the University of California, Berkeley in 2022.
