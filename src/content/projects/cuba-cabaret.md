The Cuba Cabaret was a sold out, theatrical event meant to build political consciousness and revolutionary optimism by exploring the people centered methods of resistance Cuba has adopted while enduring a US blockade of 60+ years. Part political education around Latin American colonization, part colorful, queer cabaret, the Cuba Cabaret raised $5k to send medical supplies to Cuba through the Hatuay Project. This work was inspired by Mylo's research from their participation in a Cuban Solidarity Brigade in early 2023.
