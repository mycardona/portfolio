* Mylo facilitated 30+ local bay area queer artists to come together in 2022 to build a theater from scratch in the gallery of Oakland Secret. This space was home to the story of the faggots adapted from Larry Mitchell’s, “The F*ggots and Their Friends Between Revolutions.” 
* In a world run by the evil men without color, the f*ggots and their friends are a mystical, underground people. Their ideals are the men’s fears: community care, love and a refusal to conform. 
* As you enter their world: “Marshap” (named after activist and icon Marsha P. Johnson) the faggots share seven short performances pieces,` structured according to a series of morals, or “Wisdoms,” that the classic book teaches us. Each Wisdom performed in a different performance style, ranging genres like puppetry, dance, clowning, drag, song, and more. Throughout, you also follow Lilac and Loose Tomato, two f*ggots on the same journey as many of us: figuring out how to be a good community member in a messy world and all its capitalist temptations.
* After a sold-out run at the downtown DIY venue, this production was invited to revive the show at Oakland Theater Project.
